# ☕ Khanh's Pixel Café

Tiny pixel games about tiramisu. Play in your browser, post your score, and try to take the top spot on the leaderboard.

**▶ Play now:** https://quynhkhanh96.github.io/pixel-cafe/

## On the menu

| Game | What you do | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tiramisu Factory** | Cups ride a looping conveyor belt. Drop ladyfinger, espresso, mascarpone and toppings from six machines in the right order. Khanh eats every finished cup. | Tap a machine, or keys `1`–`6` |

### Still in the oven

- **Tiramisu Rain**: catch falling slices, dodge broccoli.
- **Khanh's Bakery**: stack tiramisu layers in recipe order.
- **Tiramisu for Two**: Khanh and Petit chat noir cook as a team.

## Tiramisu Factory tips

- Watch the bubble above each cup. It shows the ingredient that cup needs next.
- When a machine glows green with ▼, tap it. The ingredient will land right in the cup.
- Wrong ingredients just bounce off. You only lose a heart when a cup runs out of loops (the dots on its rim).
- Every finished cup in a row grows your streak and your score.

## Leaderboard

Each game has its own top 10 on the café's front page. At the end of a run, enter a nickname and press **Post score**. Nicknames are up to 16 characters. Please play fair and keep nicknames friendly.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, drawn pixel by pixel on a canvas. No game engine. Fonts: [Pixelify Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pixelify+Sans) and [VT323](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/VT323).
